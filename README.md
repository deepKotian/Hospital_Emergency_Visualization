**Hospital Emergency Room Report Dashboard**

This project presents an interactive Excel-based dashboard for analyzing hospital emergency room data on a monthly and yearly basis. It provides key insights into patient counts, wait times, satisfaction scores, admissions, demographics, and departmental distribution.

**📊 Dashboard Features**

Patient Count – Displays the total number of patients treated in the selected period.
Average Wait Time – Shows the mean wait time for patients.
Satisfaction Score – Reflects overall patient satisfaction.
Admission Status – Breakdown of admitted vs. not admitted patients.
Wait Time Status – Percentage of patients treated on-time vs. delayed.
Gender Wise Count – Distribution of patients by gender.
Age Wise Count – Categorization of patients across different age groups.
Department Wise Patient Count – Distribution of patients across hospital departments.

**🧮 Filters**

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:
Year (e.g., 2023, 2024)
Month (Jan–Dec)

These filters allow users to view performance trends dynamically.

**🚀 How to Use**

Download and open the Hospital_ER_Report.xlsx file.
Use the Year and Month slicers on the left panel to filter data.
View the interactive charts and metrics for detailed insights.

**🛠 Tools Used**

Microsoft Excel → Data preparation, PivotTables, and Dashboard creation.
Charts & Slicers → For interactivity and visualization.

**📌 Insights**

Average patient wait time is ~36 minutes.
49.83% of patients were admitted, while 50.17% were not.
61% of patients were treated on time.
Patient demographics show the highest counts in 20–39 years age groups.
Most patients were seen in General Practice, followed by Gastroenterology and Orthopedics.
